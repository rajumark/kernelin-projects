Why a trading desk cares

Exotics have no formula

Barrier, Asian and lookback payoffs depend on the whole price path. Monte Carlo is the industry method, and it needs millions of paths for a stable price and stable greeks.

Risk is many reprices

Every greek is another full simulation. A book of hundreds of trades, repriced on every market move, is exactly the workload banks buy GPU farms for.

Without a second language

Usually this means CUDA or C++ kernels next to the Kotlin service. Here the pricer is one Kotlin function: tested on the JVM, run on any GPU through WebGPU.
